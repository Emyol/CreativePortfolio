# Content inbox

Drop raw material here. Nothing in this folder is published; it is the pile the content
model and site are built from. Messy is fine. Keep a photo and its note under the same
base name, for example `2025-pycon-ph.jpg` and `2025-pycon-ph.md`.

Files inside the subfolders are **git-ignored on purpose**, so raw photos and notes never
reach a remote by accident. Material is only committed once you decide to publish it and
it is moved into the site (for example `public/`).

If you do not remember a detail, leave it out. Nothing gets invented.

## Folders

| Folder | Put here | Useful details in the note |
|---|---|---|
| `events/` | Events you **attended** | name, date, place, who you went with, one moment you remember, what you took away |
| `music/` | Playing, listening, arranging, editing | what it is, when you got into it, a link or title, why it matters to you |
| `hobbies/` | Things you do or make outside school and work | what it is, how long, a thing you are proud of, photos of the thing itself |
| `people/` | Friends, orgs, communities, teachers | name and role (only with their OK), how you know them, a story |
| `portraits/` | Photos of you, any mood | where and when; candid beats posed |

## Photo tips

- JPEG, PNG or WebP. The editor later accepts up to 4 MB each, so export at web size.
- Landscape and portrait both welcome; do not crop in advance.
- Check a photo with other people in it is fine to publish.
- Do not include anything private (IDs, tickets with barcodes, addresses, phone numbers).

## Note shape (copy as a starting point)

```md
# <short title>

- When:
- Where:
- With:
- Role: attended / member / fan / maker

The moment I remember:

What I'd tell a friend about it:
```
