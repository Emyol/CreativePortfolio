# New direction: what the strongest 2025-2026 sites do (not East Asian, not isometric)

Looking for: a fresh art direction for the whole creative portfolio, replacing Kakejiku and
every concept that followed it (the cel-shaded room, the jeepney, the save point). Sources:
Awwwards 2025 annual winners (Site of the Year: Lando Norris; Developer SOTY: Messenger),
Awwwards Portfolio Honors 2024-2026, the Awwwards portfolio list as of Oct 2026, and Muzli's
2026 top-100 portfolios. Captured 2026-10-17 with headless Chromium at 1440x900: one shot at
load, one 0.4 s later, one after ~3,000 px of scroll and one after ~9,000 px. **Stills only.
Every motion line is unverified, owner to watch.** Nine sites timed out under software WebGL
(Olha Lazarieva, Emma Portner, Karma Worlds, Gianluca Gradogna, Maria Vasilyeva, Joseph San,
Lean Fourie, Sam Goddard, and Antoine Wodniack's lower sections) and are not judged.

## The pattern across the winners

1. **One owned object carries the page.** A rendered thing, not a gradient: a glass prism,
   an inflated leather "O", a chrome smiley, a shelf of helmets. The site is that object plus
   type.
2. **Two colours and a lot of nothing.** Off-white or near-black, one loud accent, if any.
3. **Type at poster scale** with tiny functional labels; almost no mid-size text.

## Lando Norris (OFF+BRAND): Awwwards Site of the Year 2025

- Link: https://landonorris.com/ (via https://www.awwwards.com/annual-awards/winners)
- Why it works: a person's site built on **his own collectibles**. His helmets sit in a grid
  of tabbed cards like trophies, each one a 3D object with a name and year. Acid lime
  (#D2FF00) on off-white and near-black, faint topographic lines, an editorial serif quote.
- How it's built: Webflow, GSAP, WebGL (Awwwards tags); the helmet cards are rendered objects.
- Steal: **a person's real things, rendered as a collection**: one object per entry, a
  label and a year under each.
- Avoid: the faint grey-on-white hero, which is nearly empty at load.
- Fit: both. Motion: **unverified**, owner to watch.

## NOTHIN' (Thomas Carré and team): SOTD Aug 10 2026, Portfolio Honors Jul 2026

- Link: https://www.noth.in/
- Why it works: the loader is the wordmark **N O '** where the **O is a real object that
  keeps changing material** while a counter runs down (an inflated leather ring at 096, a
  black chrome asterisk at 088). Then the full word lands as a giant black-on-white poster.
- How it's built: Webflow, GSAP, WebGL, hero shaders (Awwwards tags); the objects look
  pre-rendered or real-time PBR (inferred).
- Steal: **one letter of the name as a swappable rendered object**: the signature move in
  one gesture.
- Avoid: a loader you must wait through on every visit.
- Fit: technique. Motion: **unverified**, owner to watch (material swap seen across stills).

## Poolsuite

- Link: https://poolsuite.net/
- Why it works: a music site that is **an old desktop**: a draggable player window, a dock
  of pixel icons (Player, Mixtapes, Events, Guestbook), a date strip set in 1997. Music is
  the interface, not a section.
- How it's built: windowed UI in the DOM, VHS video in the player.
- Steal: music as the operating surface; a "guestbook" for the people section.
- Avoid: pastiche of an existing OS; nostalgia as the whole idea.
- Fit: voice. Motion: **unverified**.
